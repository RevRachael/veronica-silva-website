About

Spirit, science, and a lot of heart

Rev. Veronica Silva is the Senior Minister at Pacific Coast Center for Spiritual Living.

With a deep commitment to spiritual growth and personal transformation, Rev. Veronica is passionate about exploring the intersections of spirituality, emotional intelligence, and mental liberation. Having navigated the challenges of getting sober in 1996, she is a strong advocate for shadow work and believes that the integration of spiritual and emotional intelligence fosters profound inner freedom, creating space for the Divine to express through and as one's life.

Rev. Veronica brings over 15 years of experience as a Registered Nurse, including her role as Charge Nurse of a hospital COVID unit during the height of the pandemic from 2020 to 2021. She actively promoted a mindfulness program for the nurses at her hospital to support their wellbeing. In addition to her healthcare career, she successfully operated a beloved yoga studio, FreeSpirit Yoga, in Long Beach, California.

Offerings

Three ways to journey together

01

Workshop Facilitator

What happens in you when someone tells you you're wrong? How do you stay open to what they're saying while honoring what's true for you? These are the kinds of questions I bring into a workshop.

I weave short teachings with guided reflection, paired conversations, and group dialogue. In workshops like You Are Wrong, we notice what happens when we feel misunderstood, get curious about the assumptions we make, and practice listening across differences. You get to choose how much you share. The practice is something you can take into your next conversation, whether it's at home, at work, or in your community.

02

Spiritual Coach

Sometimes you need a place to say what you've been carrying around. Sometimes you know what matters to you and want support living it more fully. I offer spiritual coaching for both kinds of moments.

As your guide, I listen deeply and help you notice patterns in how you think, respond, and relate to others. Together, we get curious about those patterns and explore steps you can take toward your own freedom. I offer simple practices to help you stay present and grounded in difficult situations, so you have more room to choose how you want to respond.

03

Speaker

I find some of my best material in the middle of everyday life. A conversation that goes sideways, a moment when I catch myself doing the very thing I said I'd never do. There's depth in those moments, usually some humor, and often a place where healing can begin.

When I speak, I explore what it means to be both human and divine. I invite us to dig a little deeper, tell the truth about what it's like to be human, and laugh gently at ourselves along the way. What I teach is something I'm still practicing myself: how to love ourselves in the middle of becoming.
